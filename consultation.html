Book a Consultation

Whether you need advice on a new matter or representation in a pending case, the first step is a consultation with one of our advocates. Consultations can be held in person at any of our offices or online.

How a matter proceeds

  1. 01.

    Consultation

    We meet to understand your situation, the facts and what you hope to achieve.

  2. 02.

    Review

    We examine your documents and the applicable law, and identify the issues involved.

  3. 03.

    Strategy

    We explain your options, including settlement or mediation where suitable, and the likely course of the matter.

  4. 04.

    Drafting and filing

    Once instructed, we prepare and file the necessary pleadings, petitions or documents.

  5. 05.

    Appearance and follow-up

    We represent you before the court or authority and keep you informed at every stage.
