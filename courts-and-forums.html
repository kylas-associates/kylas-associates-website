Courts and Forums

With offices in Kochi, Thiruvananthapuram and Delhi, Kylas Associates appears before courts, tribunals and authorities across Kerala, in Delhi and in Bengaluru.

01.

Supreme Court and High Courts

  • Supreme Court of India — special leave petitions, civil and criminal appeals, writ petitions and transfer petitions. Our founder, Dr. K. P. Kylasanatha Pillay, is a designated Senior Advocate of the Supreme Court.
  • High Court of Kerala, Ernakulam — writ petitions, civil and criminal appeals and revisions, bail applications, petitions to quash proceedings, and original petitions arising from tribunal orders.
  • High Court of Delhi — writ petitions, civil and criminal matters and appeals, handled through our Delhi office.
  • High Court of Karnataka, Bengaluru — writ petitions, criminal petitions including bail and quashing, and civil appeals and revisions.
02.

Tribunals

  • Kerala Administrative Tribunal, Thiruvananthapuram and Ernakulam Benches — service matters of State Government employees.
  • Central Administrative Tribunal, Ernakulam Bench — service matters of Central Government employees.
  • Tribunal for Local Self Government Institutions — appeals and revisions against decisions of panchayats and municipalities.
  • Tax tribunals and authorities — the Income Tax Appellate Tribunal, the GST Appellate Tribunal, and the GST and income tax assessing and appellate authorities.
  • Consumer Disputes Redressal Commissions — District, State and National Commissions, in consumer complaints and appeals.
  • Kerala Real Estate Regulatory Authority (K-RERA) — complaints by homebuyers and apartment owners' associations against promoters.
03.

Trial Courts

  • Ernakulam and Thiruvananthapuram districts — District and Sessions Courts, Sub Courts, Munsiff Courts, and Chief Judicial Magistrate and Judicial First Class Magistrate Courts.
  • Kannur district — District and Sessions Courts at Thalassery.
  • Bengaluru — City Civil and Sessions Courts, and Chief Metropolitan Magistrate and Metropolitan Magistrate Courts.
04.

Family Courts

  • Family Courts in Kerala — Ernakulam and Thiruvananthapuram districts.
  • Family Courts in Delhi — divorce, maintenance, custody and guardianship matters before the Family Courts in Delhi's district court complexes.
05.

Authorities and Other Forums

  • Revenue authorities (Revenue Divisional Officers, District Collectors and the Land Revenue Commissioner) — land conversion and data bank matters.
  • Department of Mining and Geology — quarrying permits, transit passes and related proceedings.
  • Co-operative Department (Joint Registrars and Co-operative Arbitration Courts) — society disputes, elections and surcharge proceedings.
  • Sub-Registrar Offices and the Registrar of Societies — registration of property documents, wills, societies and trusts.
  • Trade Marks Registry — trademark filings and oppositions.
  • Mediation centres — attached to the High Court and the district courts, and private mediation through our MCPC-trained mediators.
