Kylas Associates

Advocates and legal consultants appearing before the Supreme Court of India, the High Court of Kerala, the High Court of Delhi, the High Court of Karnataka, tribunals, and the courts of Ernakulam, Thiruvananthapuram and Bengaluru.

Founded by Dr. K. P. Kylasanatha Pillay, Senior Advocate, Kylas Associates is a law firm with offices in Kochi, Thiruvananthapuram and Delhi. The firm handles civil, criminal, constitutional, service, education, family and regulatory matters, together with registration, conveyancing and mediation.

About the Firm

Kylas Associates was founded in 1991 by Dr. K. P. Kylasanatha Pillay, now a designated Senior Advocate practising before the Supreme Court of India. Over the years, the firm has grown into a team of eleven advocates supported by dedicated office staff, with offices in Kochi, Thiruvananthapuram and Delhi.
